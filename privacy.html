This policy explains what information My Cozy Spa ("the game") uses. We, the developer, do not run any servers for the game and do not collect your personal information ourselves.

Game progress

  • Your levels, stars, coins, diamonds, purchases and settings are saved only on your device.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We cannot see or access your game progress.
  • Deleting the game deletes this saved progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional reward videos (for example, watch a short video to get diamonds or extra time). These videos are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads.

  • On iPhone, you will be asked whether the game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • Where required (for example in the EU/UK), Google's consent message lets you choose how your data is used for ads.
  • You can change tracking at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Google's use of data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases (diamond packs, the Starter Pack and the Weekly Pass) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use analytics tools of our own.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Email mullak0609@gmail.com.
