About the game

My Cozy Spa is a relaxing salon-management game for iPhone. Drag guests to massage beds, barber chairs, saunas and mud baths, take payments at the desk, hire staff and decorate your spa.

  • 100 levels across four places: City Spa, Seaside Spa, Desert Oasis and Mountain Lodge.
  • Daily gifts, daily tasks, treasure chests and weekly events.
  • Hire staff, unlock new treatments and decorate every room.
