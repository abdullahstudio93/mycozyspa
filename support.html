Common questions

How do I play?
Drag a waiting guest to the place they need, or tap the guest and then the place. Tap the desk to take payments.

I bought something but didn't get it.
Open Settings in the game (the gear on the map) and tap Restore purchases. Diamond packs are delivered once; if they are missing, email us with the date of purchase.

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your iPhone. Deleting the game removes it.

No sound?
Check that your iPhone's silent switch is off and that sound is on in the game's settings.

How do I stop personalized ads?
Go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn it off for My Cozy Spa.
